# Obed Mortey

**Pharmacist (BPharm) · MSc Clinical Epidemiology · Pharmacoepidemiology and real-world evidence**
St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada

I work on medication use, drug safety and chronic disease in older adults.
Four years of hospital and community pharmacy practice in Ghana came first —
medication reconciliation, deprescribing reviews, adverse drug reaction
reporting — and that clinical grounding is what I bring to the data. My
published research examines multimorbidity and its social, behavioural and
genetic determinants in a 30,097-participant Canadian cohort; my current work
applies the same causal-inference toolkit to polypharmacy, potentially
inappropriate prescribing and frailty.

### Currently

Building an end-to-end observational study of polypharmacy, potentially
inappropriate medication use (Beers 2023, STOPP/START) and frailty in older
adults on a publicly available dataset — a reproducible R extraction pipeline
over roughly 7,800 variables, a machine-readable variable dictionary, and a
DAG-informed analysis with propensity score weighting and multiple imputation.
